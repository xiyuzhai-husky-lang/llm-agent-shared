# Consolidation principles

These principles apply to every role in every consolidation project.

- Everything follows the Outcome's requirements.
- Much on the Lean side is secondary: it may be temporarily broken or left as legacy, as long as a comment or another marker says so clearly. Transpiling to a Lean name that does not exist yet is allowed as a clearly marked placeholder; otherwise the work cannot be split.
- Touch the Lean side as little as possible: change only what the user asks for and what the Outcome exposes.
- Soundness is not our top priority. Our strategy is to achieve soundness asymptotically; the Lean side mainly serves to expose soundness bugs in our solver.
- While iterating fast, do not worry about so much.
